04 / A little clarity

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Is this a chatbot or an AI employee?

“AI employee” is our shorthand for a configured agent workflow, not a human replacement or an employment relationship. We build Hermes-powered systems with tools, business context, recurring jobs and review rules. The scope is defined around specific tasks.

Can it really work 24/7?

Workflows can be scheduled around the clock on an always-on host. Availability depends on hosting, integrations and model providers. We agree monitoring and recovery needs during scoping; we don't promise uninterrupted service or guaranteed results.

Will it publish or contact people without me?

Not by default. Approval gates are agreed before deployment. We can prepare content and outreach for review, with any external actions limited to the permissions you explicitly approve.

What does a setup cost?

We scope the first workflow on the call and provide a proposal before work starts. Build costs, ongoing management, hosting, model usage and third-party subscriptions are separated so you know what you're paying for.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Bring the repetitive job you want solved and the tools you already use. We handle the technical setup and walk you through how to review outputs and control the workflow.

Is Jobudo part of Nous Research?

No. This is an independent setup and managed-workflow service. Hermes Agent is developed by Nous Research. Its name and logo identify the underlying technology, not an endorsement or partnership.
